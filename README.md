Project Overview

This project analyzes 14 days of sales and stock data for Wanjiku's produce stall at Gikomba Market. Using core Python, the project calculates total and average sales, compares weekly performance, classifies daily sales, compares weekday and weekend performance, identifies products requiring replenishment, and calculates gross profit margins. The objective is to provide useful insights that can support better sales and inventory management decisions.

Top Three Findings

Total sales: KES 46,450 across the 14-day period.

Weekly growth: Week 2 sales increased by 10.66% compared with Week 1.

Weekend performance: Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared with weekday average sales of KES 2,685.

Conclusion

The analysis provides insights into sales performance, weekly growth, customer purchasing patterns, stock management, and product profitability. These findings can help Wanjiku make better purchasing and inventory decisions.
